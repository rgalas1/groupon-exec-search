The Architecture

ASSESSMENT TIMELINE

Stage 01

Executive Screening

Talent Partner • 45 Minutes
  • Are they genuinely operating at Adoptive AI fluency, or just using buzzwords?
  • Have they actually led a tech-first commercial transformation from the front?
Stage 02

Chronological Topgrade

External Search Partner • 90 Minutes
  • Walking through every role: what did they inherit, what did they build, and who did they hire/fire?
  • Digging into their specific playbook for managing a team of 20 through a massive strategic pivot.
Stage 03

Product & Strategy Lock

Chief Product Officer • 60 Minutes
  • Can they spar with product leadership on how to monetize AI features?
  • Do they understand merchant friction well enough to influence the roadmap?
