THE ARCHITECTURE

ASSESSMENT TIMELINE

STAGE 1 • 45 MIN

Executive Screening

Interviewer: Talent Partner
  • Screening for basic technical aptitude and history of scaling teams.
  • Slope Probe: "Walk me through the last workflow you fundamentally changed because of new tech."
STAGE 2 • 60 MIN

Hiring Manager Alignment

Interviewer: Chief Product Officer
  • Can they genuinely collaborate with Product to redesign the revenue motion?
  • Conceptual AI Fluency: How they think about build vs. buy, measuring AI ROI, and the risks of automation.
STAGE 3 • 90 MIN

Chronological Topgrading Deep Dive

Interviewer: Executive Panel (COO / HR Leader)
  • Walking through their career history to validate their track record of scaling teams.
  • Tracking Slope: Mapping their technology adoption arc over time. Do they lead transformations or inherit them?
STAGE 4 • 90 MIN

AI-Native Case Study & Strategy (The Fusion)

Interviewer: CPO & Key Stakeholders
  • The Prompt: Design the 2027 merchant onboarding motion assuming AI agents are a first-class part of the team.
  • Adoptive vs Capable: We assess if they fundamentally redesign the architecture (Adoptive) or just sprinkle AI on top (Capable).
  • Red Flag: Mentions ChatGPT strictly as a personal productivity hack.
  • Push Back Rule: If they only score 'Capable', push back—Groupon needs an architect who can build the transformation, not just maintain it.
STAGE 5 • 45 MIN

Final Executive Culture Fit

Interviewer: CEO / Select C-Suite
  • How they align with Groupon's broader corporate vision and culture.
  • Where they anticipate the most resistance in automating onboarding, and how they will handle it.
THE MANDATE

PERFORMANCE PROFILE

01
Successfully transition the revenue growth motion to an AI-driven sales and merchant onboarding model within the first 6 months.
02
Scale and upskill a 20-person commercial team to operate at Adoptive AI fluency.
03
Deliver a minimum of 25% increase in merchant onboarding efficiency using automated AI solutions.
04
Establish cross-functional alignment with Product to ensure the commercial strategy matches the technology-led transformation.
